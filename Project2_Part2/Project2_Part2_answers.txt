8. Comment on whether you expect R1s and R2s to be adapter-trimmed at different rates and why.
Generally, there is not a big difference between my forward and reverse reads in both my SRR datasets.
That being said, I looked into my slurm script output for trimmomatic and have the survival rates per read type:
SRR25630296: Forward Only Surviving: 222531 (0.51%) Reverse Only Surviving: 139322 (0.32%)"
SRR25630382: Forward Only Surviving: 48948 (0.70%) Reverse Only Surviving: 16611 (0.24%)
Both my datasets have a lower number of reverse strands only surviving compared to forward only surviving. I did some research and 
this is due to the default settings within trimmomatic dropping the reverse read if the insert size is below the threshold without the adapter sequence
assuming the short insert has its reverse comp sequence preserved in the forward read.
