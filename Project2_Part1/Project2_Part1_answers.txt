Overall, both the forward and reverse reads between my two SRR datasets are very similar based on the analyses performed by FastQC.
All the files are consistent in what FastQC flagged too- with per base sequence content having irregularities withint the first 10 bases suggesting that all the RNA sequences share a sequence motif in the beginning likely due to a primer bonding region or barcode. 
All the files also were flagged for sequence duplication levels which is consistent with RNA since certain transcripts will be repeated due to being upregulated.
Of note, all the files except SRR25630296_2.fastq were flagged for per tile sequence quality which could be worth looking into.
